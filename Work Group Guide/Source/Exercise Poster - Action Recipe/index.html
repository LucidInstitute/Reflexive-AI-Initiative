Human-centered AI — Action Recipe

Describe one concrete action an organization can take to keep AI human-centered and govern it properly — clearly enough that someone outside your group could pick it up.

// Theme
// Name of the action
Write slowly and in capitals. So we understand the recipe later.
Be concrete. Name roles, steps and moments rather than general intentions.
Keep it non-sensitive. Avoid personal data and business-confidential information.
Type of action Awareness Policy Structure Workflow Training & skills Tooling Decision Strategy Other:
1
Aim
What is the aim?
  • Which undesirable outcome does it prevent?
  • Which desirable outcome does it encourage?
2
People
Who needs to be involved?
  • Who performs the action, and who decides?
  • Who is affected, and how do they get a voice?
3
Steps
What should happen?
  • What are the concrete steps, and in what order?
  • What is needed to make it happen (time, budget, tools, mandate)? When does it start?
4
Success
When is it successful?
  • Which signals, indicators or evidence show it works?
  • Who checks this, and how often?
