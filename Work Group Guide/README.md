# Reflexive AI Work Groups
The Reflexive AI Work Groups iteratively build a governance framework through a process of participatory action research and design science. Each Work Group consists of three 3-hour sessions seperated by a month (or more) to allow participants to take action inbetween.

The outcomes of these sessions are anonymized, and then submitted to our research initiative for further analyses.

## Requirements
- 5-30 participants, though we provide options for when you have fewer.
- Aim for a diverse group, as bringing many perspectives together makes World Cafes much more powerful.
- Facilitation experience, particularly with Liberating Structures, is highly recommended.
- A space where you can create five or six "stations"; tables with chairs and/or with flipcharts.
