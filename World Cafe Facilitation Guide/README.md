# Reflexive AI World Cafe
The Reflexive AI World Cafe is a 2-hour workshop that can be run in any organization or meetup. A World Cafe is a format where small groups discuss a deep question, and then rotate to the next question. It asks participants to reflect on what "human-centered AI" means to them and this organization. The World Cafe closes with concrete follow-up actions generated by participants to make AI use in your organization more human-centered. Our facilitation guide details the steps and instructions in a handy step-by-step fashion.

The anonymized outcomes are scrubbed of business-sensitive details, and then submitted to our research initiative for further analyses. That way, the World Cafe generates useful insights for your organization while also contributing insights to our research initiative (a win-win).

## Requirements
- 15-25 participants, though we provide options for when you have fewer.
- Aim for a diverse group, as bringing many perspectives together makes World Cafes much more powerful.
- Some facilitation experience helps.
- A space where you can create five or six "stations"; tables with chairs and/or with flipcharts.
