Reflexive AI Workgroup

Session results: the questions each theme group believes organizations should answer to keep AI human-centered, and the action recipes they drafted in response.

// Session
November 13, 2026 · Amersfoort (NL) · Session #2
12 participants, plus 2 facilitators; 2 new participants joined and 1 participant from Session #1 could not attend. Formats: question storming with 1-2-4-All and action recipes for five themes, refined through Shift & Share, followed by 15% Solutions. The group worked mostly in Dutch; responses are shown in the original with an English translation.
