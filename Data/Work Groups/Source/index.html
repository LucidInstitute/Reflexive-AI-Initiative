Premortem · Shift & Share

Meaningful Work

1 · Failure mode
“Imagine what it would look like if AI was not human-centered for this theme. What would happen, or not happen?”
# Response
1 Humans will just review what AI does
2 AI takes all the challenging work, so humans will not be relevant anymore
3 AI takes over all the creative and meaningful work
4 We humans stop evolving and learning
5 Through AI we lose our professional identity
6 Increased anxiety and stress at work because of the sense of being replaceable
7 Effort feels pointless (why should I try if AI does it better?) – lack of motivation
8 We develop and use AI technology that doesn't necessarily serve real human needs
9 AI provides answers and recommendations, but the underlying processes are not transparent. As a result, people cannot fully understand, question, or verify how decisions are made
10 Risk of losing trust, control, and human autonomy
Note: follows from #9
11 Confidence will be lost: "I can't do it without AI"
2 · Prevention / actions
“What can we do to prevent undesirable outcomes from occurring and encourage desirable outcomes to happen?”
# Response
12 Explicit delegation and role definitions to clarify the boundaries between AI and human tasks
Note: written in response to #2
13 Rethink job and role design
14 Involve employees in defining those new roles in the AI era
15 Accept a slower pace to keep the human learning loop alive
16 Design the whole system to avoid AI taking the most impactful work
17 Define specific AI competencies for every system around us (education, organizations, government)
18 What happens if AI is not available? "Disaster & recovery" policies
19 Make failure part of our culture, since it's part of the learning process we need as humans. AI also makes mistakes

Premortem · Shift & Share

Workforce & Learning

1 · Failure mode
“Imagine what it would look like if AI was not human-centered for this theme. What would happen, or not happen?”
# Response
1 Without learning, there is no human center
2 People are mandated to use AI in pre-determined & rigid ways, divorced from the actual work needed to support the customer
3 In Tayloristic & Neo-Welchist organizations this is already happening
4 The outcome is staff frustration, disengagement, loss of skills & staff disgruntlement
Note: follows from #2
5 This will lead to workplace unrest
6 Management lays people off, revenue falls, customers desert the firm, and the economy tanks
7 Reverse centaurism
2 · Prevention / actions
“What can we do to prevent undesirable outcomes from occurring and encourage desirable outcomes to happen?”
# Response
8 Allow staff to discover among themselves how best to use automation to benefit the customer and solve work problems
9 Allow staff to dialogue with management on how end-to-end processes need to adapt, and on what is not wise or possible to automate
10 Firm-wide facilitated dialogue in an open spirit, where management is open to learning from staff & staff is key
11 Staff understanding the larger business environment & committing to transparency
12 Ensure through training that critical thinking, quality and skills are maintained
13 Enable unions where none exist, to balance worker power with management
14 Staff must have ownership of the work & the standards
15 Disaggregation & leaderless work may need to be negotiated
16 Mindset is key: leaders must role-model & encourage the mindset change, beginning with themselves
17 Feedback with Radical Candor in a psychologically safe environment. There is a big role for management here

“What can we do to prevent undesirable outcomes from occurring and encourage desirable outcomes to happen?”
# Response

18 Capacity building: training for low-maturity users + a narrative on the impact of AI
19 Mandatory reporting on AI usage
20 Useful & mindful consumption: choosing sustainable AI providers (e.g. Ecosia AI)

Premortem · Shift & Share

Risk & Boundaries

1 · Failure mode
“Imagine what it would look like if AI was not human-centered for this theme. What would happen, or not happen?”
# Response
1 Right now, not working in a human-centered way → incidents that show no proper risk management is happening
2 Misuse of AI (excessive, non-regulated use; confusion of responsibility)
3 Not using AI (strict regulations → employees start secretly using AI, which can get out of control)
4 Misunderstanding of how work results or feedback were achieved (e.g., HR uses AI to select applicants – no transparency concerning rejections)
2 · Prevention / actions
“What can we do to prevent undesirable outcomes from occurring and encourage desirable outcomes to happen?”
# Response
5 Stay adaptive: be flexible regarding the needs users have, new learnings and new guidelines → monitor and adapt
6 Find a balance between giving people the time to get used to tools/systems, while being aware that the first choice may not always be the best
Note: elaborates on #5
7 Don't choose the cheapest/easiest/best-known tool/system → analyze BEFORE what people need
8 Sanction shadow/parallel systems (i.e., how do we cope if people use parallel systems?) → should also be set by the government
9 Build psychological safety (to avoid finger-pointing)
10 Implement a reporting system without fear of repercussions (i.e., where can I report if something goes wrong?)
11 Awareness training on what it means in daily life; be aware of risks
12 Developers should also consider safety/boundaries (e.g., AI tells you when you ask a question outside the boundaries for this organization: "this is not going to happen because of this and that policy we have at this organization")
