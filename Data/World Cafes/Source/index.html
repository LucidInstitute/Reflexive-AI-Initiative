Station 5

Measurement & auditing

“What could be measured, tracked or audited to ensure that the way your organization uses AI is human-centered?”
# Response Votes
3 Criteria are Asimov’s laws: harm avoidance, obedience, self-protection 6
5 Auditing: what does human-centered AI mean ⇒ shared understanding in the organization 5
13 Measure psychological safety in relation to working with AI: How safe do you feel sharing personal data with the AI? 4
17 What percentage of the time can an employee pursue the core of their work? E.g., how much time does the nurse have to care for patients? 4
11 Metric for the freedom/free space and creativity that arise through human-centered AI 3
9 Employee satisfaction: How satisfied are you with our AI (the way we use AI)? Would you work in this organization again, the way it uses AI? Also conceivable as customer satisfaction 2
10 Accountability framework: which agent is responsible for which task/process, and who is responsible for the agent (a human!)? 1
14 Better quality; more time for creativity; change in lead/turnaround times. Label work (tickets) processed with AI. Measure the company’s innovation capability 1
15 How many fewer workplace accidents have occurred thanks to AI? 1
16 Data protection and information security: agree on the recording of meetings and don’t pressure others 1
18 Work vs. employment/occupation 1
1 Aligned with the EU AI Act or comparable national commitments 0

4 What feedback do I get from the AI? 0
6 Scanning for “inhuman” actions by the AI, and also for nonsense 0
7 Tracking “patterns” to recognize whether AI is still working in a human-centered way 0
8 Deriving goals ⇒ deriving metrics & key figures 0

Action

15% Solutions

Actions participants can take without extra permission or resources.
# Response
1 PROMOTE / ADVOCATE for human-centered work models
2 Bring the concept of human-centered AI into the discourse of the org units. Become an HC-AI ambassador.
3 Consistently outsource routine tasks to AI and use the freed-up time for creativity and exchange
4 Show empathy for colleagues
5 I – not the AI – decide whether the problem has been solved
6 Share my experiences with AI; give space for exchange
7 Be open to new things
8 I can discuss the results of the evening with IT’s AI “officer/representative”
9 I only use AI tools approved by my company
10 Share knowledge
11 Continue learning with/about AI
12 Four-eyes principle with the AI; risk analysis
13 Leadership (category label for the next item)
14 General check on whether the use of this AI is still within the target corridor
