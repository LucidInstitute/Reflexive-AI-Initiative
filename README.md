# Reflexive AI Research Initiative

Welcome to the repository of the [Reflexive AI Research Initiative](https://reflexiveai.org/). We are a collaborative action research project run by volunteers to uncover how organizations can harness the power of AI effectively, responsibly and in a manner that empowers employees and teams.

The purpose of this repository is to share outcomes, intermediate results and facilitation materials that are part of the research process.
