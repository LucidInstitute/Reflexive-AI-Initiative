# Reflexive AI Research Initiative

Welcome to the repository of the [Reflexive AI Research Initiative](https://reflexiveai.org/). We are a collaborative action research project run by volunteers to uncover how organizations can harness the power of AI effectively, responsibly and in a manner that empowers employees and teams.

The purpose of this repository is to share outcomes, intermediate results and facilitation materials that are part of the research process.

## Study overview

We follow an action research approach where academics collaborate with practitioners to develop a theoretical framework for governance of AI in organizations. Our guiding Research Question is "_How organizations can leverage the power of artificial intelligence in a manner that is human-centered, responsible and reflexive. What principles, rules, norms, interventions, processes and strategies are needed to achieve this? How can this be monitored?_". This question is grounded in pragmatism. While our research will develop theory, the aim of this theory is to help solve real-world challenges.

To answer this research question, professionals and academics collaborate in a series of workgroups to analyze data, develop this framework, identify interventions and test it in their own organizations. We assume that both professionals and academics are active sense makers of their environments, have equal epistemic access and collaborate as peers. This is different from a traditional scientific paradigm where qualitative data is collected from participants and then analyzed objectively by detached scientists who assign meaning to the data.

Data will be collected from a variety of sources:

- *World Cafe's*: Following [Schiele et al (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusres.2021.10.075) and [Lohr, Weinhardt & Sieber (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1177/1609406920916976) we use the "World Cafe" format as a participatory method for collecting qualitative data from different organizations. This method is consistent with the shared sensemaking action research. With a [standardized protocol](https://github.com/LucidInstitute/Reflexive-AI-Initiative/blob/main/Focus%20Group%20Guide/Focus%20Group%20Facilitation%20Guide.pdf), facilitators will run "World Cafes in different organizations and share the anonymous results with the research initiative. This will feed into the work groups and other research activities, and inform the factors, themes and areas that need to be part of a governance framework. Qualitative data will be analyzed through the Grounded Theory approach (Strauss & Corbin)
- *Surveys*: We will deploy surveys to collect qualitative and qa
